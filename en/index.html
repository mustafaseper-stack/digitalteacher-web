Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Digital Teacher ("App") is committed to protecting your privacy and respecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use our App.

1. Data Collection

When you register using "Google Sign-In", we collect basic profile information (such as your name and email address) strictly to create and authenticate your account.

2. Data Usage

The collected data is used to:

  • Create and secure your personal account.
  • Synchronize your in-app purchases and subscriptions across devices.
  • Provide customer support.

3. Third-Party Services

Our App uses trusted third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For user authentication and analytics.
  • RevenueCat: For managing secure subscriptions via Apple App Store and Google Play.

We do not store your credit card details. All payments are securely handled by Apple and Google.

4. Data Security & Deletion Rights

Your data is protected with industry-standard encryption. You can request the permanent deletion of your account and data at any time via the app settings or by contacting us.
